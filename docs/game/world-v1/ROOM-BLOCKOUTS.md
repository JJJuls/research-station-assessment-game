## Shared construction rules

The current player body is 32×42 px. Main spines 128px/4tiles, secondary lanes and doors 96px/3tiles provide 86px/54px clearance over the vertical body dimension before lateral offsets. A4×4tile pad accommodates turning and task approach. Keep required walking surfaces separate from furniture, decorative storage and foreground projections. Author collider and foot anchor per asset, not from transparent frame bounds.

A room is not a blank rectangle filled with stations. Its hull encloses functional rooms/recesses around a clear circulation system. The blockout floor is the union of spines, local loops, functional district floors, approach lanes and quiet spaces; the remaining dark mass is architecture (Yard: impassable terrain). Main spines remain open through local loops. No task object occupies a spine. Floor seams and pipe runs indicate boundaries; only actual blocked masses collide. Covered connections terminate at the existing cardinal doors.

Initial camera rectangles are authored in the JSON, clamped 40×22.5tile views. Beyond-view districts are intentional; a visible route and recognisable threshold provide continuity. Full-room diagrams are plans, not a promise that the participant sees the entire room at once.

## Production geometry gate

Geometry in the JSON is a design blockout, not an automatic conversion of every dark shape into collision. Fable must instantiate footprints, inflate by the actual player collider, prove every door→spawn→task approach route, then capture visible collision. Show the selected initial camera at both resolutions and an opposite-edge view for each large zone. Reject decorative blocking, false door silhouettes and unexplained threshold directions.
