# Station 080 rescue — direction contact sheet

Three golden-frame candidates were generated blind (same brief, three
art directions), compared at native size, and one was selected before
any production asset was made. Files in `directions/`.

| Frame                                   | Direction                                                                                                                                                                   | Verdict                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `directions/direction-a-industrial.png` | **A — "Harbour Light"**: theater-box top-down, weathered riveted steel, warm sodium work lamps against cool steel shadows, grouped cargo, snow tracked in at the vault seal | **SELECTED.** Projection matches the game's top-down sprites; density and material language hit the Graveyard-Keeper benchmark; the frame itself was strong enough to become the Dock's painted plate. |
| `directions/direction-b-clean.png`      | B — clean Antarctic survey: white/blue insulated panels, orange accents, floor wayfinding lines                                                                             | Rejected: oblique projection clashes with the character sprites. Its floor wayfinding-line idea and orange accent language were folded into A.                                                         |
| `directions/direction-c-emergency.png`  | C — emergency-lit aftermath: teal darkness, amber lamps, frost creep, snow melt                                                                                             | Rejected: isometric projection. Its emergency-power mood became the Dock's pre-check-in state (cold plate tint + dark lamp pools) executed at runtime.                                                 |

## Production consequences of the selection

- **Painted plates**: the selected frame proved a single authored
  painting reads better than any tile assembly this project produced, so
  the Dock plate IS the direction-A frame, and the Concourse plate was
  generated with it as the style reference (doorways inpainted where the
  room topology needs them).
- **Scale**: the frame's wall heights and prop sizes fixed the world
  field at 640×360 (2× at 720p, 3× at 1080p) — the previous 1280×720
  field showed the art at half this size and was the single largest
  cause of the "blockout" reading.
- **Opening**: two further key-art paintings in the same style (plateau
  establishing; shuttle at the seal) replaced the in-engine diagram map.
