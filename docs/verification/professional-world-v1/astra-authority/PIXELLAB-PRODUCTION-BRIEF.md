# Bounded PixelLab production brief

Astra specifies; Fable produces only after the owner approves the production mission and the specific asset batch. **No PixelLab call is authorised by this Astra pass.** Audit first: [existing asset register](../../../game/world-v1/ASSET-PROVENANCE-REGISTER.md).

Each atlas should be power-of-two 512/1024 where practical, with 2 px transparent padding between frames, explicit trimmed/untrimmed bounds and pivots. Atlas dimensions are packing choices, not sprite-scale changes. Do not introduce external fonts or dependencies.

## Prompt template

“Original adult research-station [functional object], straight-on top-down/three-quarter with parallel ground edges and shallow visible top, [exact source dimensions],32 px ground-tile density, upper-left light, muted blue-grey painted metal and warm paper/service accents from the approved palette. Clear [control/handling face] on south edge, base footprint[exact rectangle], transparent RGBA background. [State variant], fixed silhouette/pivot across animation. No text, no floating labels, no glow ring, no perspective diamond, no character.”

Before each batch substitute literal family/dimensions/footprint from its room blockout. Preserve prompt, returned model/version/seed metadata where available, candidate identifier, original dimensions/alpha, rejection reason and approval. Do not cite an unavailable seed or claim a human approval that did not occur.

## Promotion gate and integration order

A0 precedes room shells; A1 closes Dock/Concourse; A2–A4 close their individual zone units; A5 closes Utility/Core. Temporary procedural placeholders may support geometry checks but cannot pass the final professional-art gate. Retain current characters throughout. Decorative variety is not a new batch until architecture, landmarks, usable faces, doors and depth all pass.

Inspect each candidate at 1× and selected 1.5× output, beside the player and neighbouring kit. Check palette, front/top ratio, contour, readable operating face, floor contact, collision clearance and every state frame. Reject silhouette drift, full-frame collision, incongruent isometric faces, baked essential text, constant animation or outcome cues. One accepted variant must have a static reduced-motion equivalent. Asset version updates belong in src/constants/assets.ts without changing research export semantics.

Before each batch freeze an exact state-to-frame-count manifest (for example available 1, closed 1, settled 1, door-open 8). Ranges in this brief are planning limits, not permission for unpredictable output. Specify edge extrusion compatible with alpha, or demonstrate no atlas bleed under the chosen 1x/1.5x sampler; transparent gutters alone do not prove it.
