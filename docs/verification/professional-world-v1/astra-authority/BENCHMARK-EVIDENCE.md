# Benchmark evidence and transfer boundaries

Research date: 2026-09-08. Official pages and official gallery pixels, plus the completed local Emberville ingestion. Commercial games are game-design evidence only, never psychometric authority.

A read-only art specialist visually inspected ten actual official screenshots kept under %TEMP%/astra-world-authority-references. The citations below identify the specific images supporting each observation. Static images establish visible composition, not hidden collision, live timing, navigation code or measured usability. No reference image is included in this package.

## Graveyard Keeper — primary reference

The workyard separates equipment lots around continuous circulation: furnace/anvil work and a well occupy purposeful positions beside routes. In the graveyard, the church supplies a dominant landmark and paths divide smaller plots; one focused grave shows a prompt while the rest of the scene remains unlabelled. These are visible pixels in the [official workyard image](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/599140/ss_2edf1779ad9992da1ae8f0f982a80f8945506154.1920x1080.jpg) and [official graveyard image](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/599140/ss_ed94df58a35110c78a5de4ea20a6922a5f13aaa9.1920x1080.jpg).

The village image shows continuous stone circulation past a large ruin and a fenced house plot, with barrels/crates grouped near the boundary and buildings continuing beyond the frame. The official store links resource gathering, production and excursions into a functional work loop. [Official village image](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/599140/ss_9533ec3b7abd3300e9873dcd0d2bd5794a8fe54b.1920x1080.jpg), [developer/publisher store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/599140/Graveyard_Keeper/).

Transfer: room purpose through clustered apparatus; church-scale landmark hierarchy becomes an original station counter, archive wall or mast; continuous clear circulation; one focused affordance. Reject: copied church/graveyard/workyard geometry, macabre comic tone, economic progression, resource scarcity gates, technology trees and task-benefit changes.

## Stardew Valley

Official farm pixels show a door connected to a stepping path, separated crop plots and irregular pond/fence/vegetation boundaries. A rural-path image shows a fork bounded by pasture and trees, a sign at the junction and NPCs on the route. These illustrate thresholds, route junctions and supporting landmarks rather than a copied map. [Farm image](https://www.stardewvalley.net/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/1_1screenshot15.png), [rural path image](https://www.stardewvalley.net/wp-content/uploads/2018/12/StardewValley_4.png).

The official premise gives inherited tools a clear acquisition story. An historical developer update describes community-centre work with visible restoration and route consequences; it is a 2014 design rationale, not evidence of current exact balancing. [Official site](https://www.stardewvalley.net/), [developer update20](https://www.stardewvalley.net/dev-update-20/).

Transfer: tools originate in a credible role; optional recesses and readable thresholds; visible consequence. Reject: farming map, seasons/economy, stamina pressure, reward-dependent route restoration or day-clock burden in the assessment.

## Fields of Mistria

Official gallery screenshot0 places house steps, stepping stones and a fence opening along one readable approach to planted ground. Chest/mailbox sit beside the house. Its clock and hotbar are prominent, so it is **not** evidence for universally minimal HUD. The developer describes a town recovering after an earthquake, linking community work to a restoration premise. [Official gallery](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2142790/Fields_of_Mistria/), [developer site](https://www.fieldsofmistria.com/).

Transfer: damage, civic purpose and work form a continuous story; functional props belong near their owning building. Reject: character designs, pastel fantasy identity, exact town layout, magic/progression and celebratory rewards that train indicative behaviour.

## Sun Haven

Official pixels show brick/stone routes dividing barn/pen, garden and café functions; a cup-shaped roof sign identifies the café and a waterfall creates an edge. Dense decoration and an obscuring cloud would be unsuitable for primary task views. The official store describes extensive crafting and town-specific work; its weather option supports considering reducible presentation, not changing construct-relevant mechanics. [Official scene image](https://shared.akamai.steamstatic.com/store_item_assets/steam/apps/1432860/ss_9225c3d02fff6c0057a14176bb7c6b02a613aac8.1920x1080.jpg), [official store](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1432860/Sun_Haven/).

Transfer: architectural function and strong boundary landmarks. Reject: dense decorative variety, copied fantasy motifs, cloud occlusion, combat, skill perks, stamina/difficulty options or collecting many worktables in one assessment room.

## Design inference, not empirical validation

Across these references, circulation and landmark hierarchy make a zone's function visible; furniture groups belong to work areas, edges carry storage and room extent continues outside the view. These patterns motivate the selected larger-zone blockouts. The choice of a 40×22.5 field,128/96px paths, timing targets, five-state grammar and assessment burden gates is **our design judgement**, not a finding scientifically established by commercial games.

Original scientific papers were not available in the repository. The workbook's source-scale DOIs remain provenance in the crosswalk; no benchmark is evidence for construct validity, questionnaire equivalence or hiring utility.
